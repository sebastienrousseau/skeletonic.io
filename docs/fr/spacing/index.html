Nous travaillons à sa traduction complète.</strong> <!-- i18n-summary --></p> </blockquote> <p>The padding and margin utility classes in Skeletonic share a single <strong>five-step golden-ratio scale</strong>. Each step is rooted at <code>--gr-fsb</code> (1.62 rem, the body font size) and scales up or down by factors of Φ. The result is layout that breathes consistently from inline buttons all the way to hero sections.</p> <hr class="hr-text" data-content="Scale"> <h2>The five steps</h2> <div class="table-responsive"><table class="table"> <thead> <tr> <th>Class</th> <th align="right">Value</th> <th>Token</th> <th>Use it for</th> </tr> </thead> <tbody> <tr> <td class="text-left"><code>xs</code></td> <td align="right" class="text-right">~0.38 rem</td> <td class="text-left"><code>var(--gr-small)rem</code></td> <td class="text-left">Pill icons, badge inset, very tight clusters</td> </tr> <tr> <td class="text-left"><code>sm</code></td> <td align="right" class="text-right">~0.62 rem</td> <td class="text-left"><code>var(--gr-large)rem</code></td> <td class="text-left">Inline form gaps, button-group separators</td> </tr> <tr> <td class="text-left"><code>md</code></td> <td align="right" class="text-right">1.62 rem</td> <td class="text-left"><code>var(--gr-fsb)rem</code></td> <td class="text-left">Default body / card padding</td> </tr> <tr> <td class="text-left"><code>lg</code></td> <td align="right" class="text-right">~2.62 rem</td> <td class="text-left"><code>calc(var(--gr-fsb) × Φ)</code></td> <td class="text-left">Section breathing room</td> </tr> <tr> <td class="text-left"><code>xl</code></td> <td align="right" class="text-right">~4.24 rem</td> <td class="text-left"><code>calc(var(--gr-fsb) × Φ²)</code></td> <td class="text-left">Hero / landmark separation</td> </tr> </tbody> </table></div> <p>Values are emitted as <strong><code>rem</code> units</strong> so they scale with the user's root font-size — perfect for accessibility and for matching the type scale exactly.</p> <hr class="hr-text" data-content="Visual"> <h2>Visual ladder</h2> <style> .spacing-row{ display:flex;
